# Vocogni Terms of Service — Working Draft

**Status:** Internal product draft — not approved for public release  
**Prepared:** 28 October 2026  
**Operator:** Not yet confirmed  
**Support / legal contact:** Not yet configured

> **Important:** This document is a product-planning draft, not legal advice or a finalized legal agreement. The operator's legal identity, contact details, applicable governing-law terms, dispute process, and required consent flows must be completed and reviewed by qualified counsel before public release. Showing this draft in the app does not itself record acceptance or establish a valid contract.

## 1. What Vocogni is

Vocogni is a developing educational learning prototype. Its current features include authored courses and practice, progress summaries, a personal notes workspace, preferences, and a limited rule-based assistant. Features and content may change as the prototype is developed.

Vocogni is a learning aid. It is not a school, examination board, accredited qualification provider, or substitute for a teacher's assessment.

## 2. Eligibility and adult oversight

Until the operator completes a child-safety assessment and implements all required age-appropriate controls and consent flows, Vocogni is intended only for controlled development testing with responsible adult oversight.

If you are under 18, use the prototype only with permission and appropriate supervision from a parent, guardian, or responsible educator. This notice is **not** a substitute for verifiable parent/guardian consent or other controls that may be legally required. The current prototype does not provide a completed guardian-consent recording workflow. Public access by children must not be treated as approved until this blocker is resolved.

## 3. Accounts and account security

Sign-in is handled through the configured identity provider. You are responsible for using a secure sign-in method and not sharing credentials or leaving an authenticated session open on a shared device. Do not attempt to access another learner's account or data.

The prototype's authentication, account isolation, recovery, and deletion processes have not yet been independently verified for production use.

## 4. Acceptable use

Use Vocogni for legitimate learning and product testing. You must not:
- access, infer, copy, or disclose another person's private information;
- impersonate another learner, bypass access controls, or interfere with the service;
- use the service to bully, threaten, harass, or exploit another person;
- submit malicious content, attempt to compromise the app, or deliberately disrupt its operation; or
- treat prototype outputs as official grades, professional advice, or a guarantee of correctness.

Respect the privacy and intellectual-property rights of others. Only submit material you are permitted to use.

## 5. Learning content and results

Course explanations, answer checking, diagnostic placement, feedback, recommendations, and progress measures may contain errors or gaps. Some features are rule-based prototypes and use authored questions or limited recorded evidence. A result may describe recorded activity; it is not necessarily a validated measure of knowledge, learning gain, ability, or future performance.

Check important answers against your teacher, course materials, or reliable references. Do not rely on Vocogni alone for consequential academic decisions.

## 6. Assistant and AI-related features

In the current repository version, the Vocogni Assistant is a deterministic, rule-based helper; it does not call an external generative-AI model. It recognizes a limited set of requests and summarizes information already available in the app. Its replies can be incomplete because its supported request set is limited.

If later versions add an external AI provider or AI-generated content, the service must update its notices before the feature is released to explain what data is sent, to whom, for what purpose, and under what provider retention/training terms. An AI label must not be used to imply a capability that is not actually implemented.

See **AI Use and Safety Notes** in the in-app Trust & Safety page.

## 7. Learner content

You retain whatever rights you already have in your own notes and content. You authorize the prototype to process submitted content only as needed to display and operate the learning features you choose. This draft does not grant the operator ownership of learner-created notes.

Do not enter passwords, government identification numbers, detailed health information, precise home addresses, financial details, private information about other people, or confidential school records. Avoid including personal details that are not needed for learning.

## 8. Data, third-party services, and availability

The current app uses an identity provider for sign-in, Streamlit for the application runtime/hosting, and Supabase PostgreSQL for durable learner-state storage. These providers may process data as part of operating the service. The operator still needs to confirm the applicable provider configurations, processing locations, retention settings, and contractual terms.

The prototype is provided for development and may be unavailable, reset, changed, or discontinued. Do not use it as the only copy of important schoolwork. The app has not been production-validated for availability, recovery, deletion, or security.

See **Privacy Notice** in the in-app Trust & Safety page.

## 9. Safety concerns and support

The current prototype has no fully configured, monitored support or safety-reporting contact. Do not put urgent or highly sensitive information into the app. For a safety concern, stop using the relevant feature and tell a parent/guardian, educator, or other trusted adult. The operator must publish a monitored contact and an incident-response process before public release.

## 10. Changes, suspension, and termination

The operator may update, restrict, or discontinue prototype features to maintain reliability and safety. A production version needs a clear notice process for material terms changes, account suspension/termination, export, and deletion.

## 11. Disclaimers and limitation of responsibility

To the extent permitted by applicable law, Vocogni is provided as a developing prototype without a guarantee that every feature, explanation, saved record, or recommendation will be accurate, uninterrupted, or suitable for a particular purpose. Nothing in this draft excludes a right or protection that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Any final warranty, liability, governing-law, complaint, and dispute-resolution terms must be written for the actual operator and reviewed by qualified counsel; this draft deliberately does not invent them.
